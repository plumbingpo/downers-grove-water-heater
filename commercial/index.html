Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as lukewarm water at the dish machine and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main supply. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, with service scheduled around your hours.

Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained kitchen to health inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Preventive Care

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Downers Grove Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water quality, usage patterns, and budget.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
